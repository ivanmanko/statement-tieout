Statement Tie-Out

Upload a bank statement. It is reconciled against its own printed totals, and what comes back is the workpaper: what is proven, what is an exception, whose exception it is, and what to do next.

or drop a PDF here — scanned statements are read too, and nothing is stored

First question

Is this the whole population?

Nothing can be sampled from a list that may be incomplete by an unknown amount. Where the statement prints transaction counts, the shortfall is exact; where it does not, the report says the population is unbounded rather than implying it is whole.

Second question

Where did each figure come from?

Every transaction carries the page and line it was read from, and every summary field records whether it was printed on the statement or derived from the rows.

Third question

Whose exception is this?

A residual is either the tool misreading the page or the statement disagreeing with itself. These are separated, because chasing a phantom reconciling item costs a day and booking one is worse.
